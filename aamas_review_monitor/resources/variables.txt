paper: the paper the message or comment is about, e.g. #7
url: that paper's forum on OpenReview
papers: the papers it is about, e.g. #7, #9
count: how many papers it is about
deadline_reviews: the review deadline
deadline_reviews_emergency: the deadline for emergency reviews (and the last one for any review)
deadline_reviews_spc: the deadline for SPCs to write the reviews still missing
deadline_meta_reviews: the meta-review deadline
deadline_acknowledgements: the deadline for acknowledging rebuttals
discussion_start: the day the reviewers' discussion opens
reviews_min: the reviews each paper needs
days_reviews: days left until the review deadline
days_reviews_emergency: days left until the emergency review deadline
days_reviews_spc: days left until the SPCs' review deadline
days_meta_reviews: days left until the meta-review deadline
days_acknowledgements: days left until the acknowledgement deadline
