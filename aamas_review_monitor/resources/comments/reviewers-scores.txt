Title: Diverging scores on paper $paper

Dear reviewers,

The ratings given to this paper diverge considerably.

Once the discussion opens on $discussion_start, after the author rebuttal, please
__read each other's reviews__ and the rebuttal, and discuss here the points on which your assessments differ.

- If the discussion changes your view:
    * please update your review and your score accordingly,
    possibly informing the SPC of the change via an official comment;
- if it does not:
    * confirm your opinion explicitly via an official comment,
    so that the SPC can weigh the different opinions in the meta-review.

Please __reply by posting an Official Comment on OpenReview__, not by answering the
notification email: replies to that email reach the Program Chairs, not us.

Thank you for your work for AAMAS.

Best regards,
Your Senior Area Chair
