Name: Review to improve (to its reviewer)
Description: A reply to each review the review rubric finds thin, readable by its reviewer
Codes: poor_review
Readers: review-authors, spcs
Title: Could you expand your review of $paper?
---
Dear $review,

Thank you for your review. To make it as useful as possible to the authors and to the SPC,
could you please expand it? In particular, a helpful review:

- summarises the paper's contribution in your own words;
- names specific strengths and weaknesses, pointing to the sections, theorems, figures or
  experiments concerned;
- suggests how the main weaknesses could be addressed;
- asks the questions whose answers could change your assessment;
- relates the work to prior work, citing it when judging novelty;
- explains how these points lead to your rating.

$issues

If you believe your review already covers these points, please disregard this comment.

Thank you for your work for AAMAS.
